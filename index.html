About

I'm an ML Research Engineer on the Recommendation and Arbitration team at Comcast, where I work on LLM post-training, data augmentation for cold-start recommendation, and large-scale search & content-discoverability systems for Xfinity. My research spans sequential recommendation, LLM fine-tuning (SFT, DPO, RLHF, knowledge distillation), and ASR evaluation, with 7 papers across venues including NAACL, EACL, CIKM, RecSys, SIGIR, ACL, and COLING, plus 2 filed patents.

I hold an M.S. in Data Science from Indiana University Bloomington and a B.S. in Computer Science from IIIT Tiruchirappalli.
