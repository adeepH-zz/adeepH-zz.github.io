Research Interests

My research centers on making large language models efficient and reliable enough to personalize at production scale. This spans three threads: (1) LLM post-training, including supervised fine-tuning, DPO, and knowledge distillation to build compact, deployable models from larger teachers; (2) generative and sequential recommendation, using LLM-generated synthetic data to address cold-start and long-tail recommendation; and (3) efficient LLM inference, building serving infrastructure that makes LLM-scale personalization affordable in production. I am interested in continuing this line of work during a PhD, with a particular focus on efficient post-training and inference methods.
